## A. Project-level design brief (paste first)
Design a compact serviceable socketed carrier PCB for one hiBCTR ESP32-S3 N16R8 development board, using an external fixed-5V AITRIP buck module, an existing 8-channel 12V-to-3.3V optocoupler module, 12 buffered pixel data channels for WS2815 12V LEDs, and an optional 10-channel smart high-side 12V switch bank. The logic PCB must NOT carry full LED supply current: use an enclosure-contained separately fused rated power bus/harness to output connectors. Preserve GPIO4..11 for eight trigger inputs and GPIO16,17 for the first two LED data channels. Provide two 74AHCT245 octal buffers on 5V, an I2C MCP23017 for switch-control GPIO expansion on 3.3V, and three TPS4H160-Q1 quad smart high-side switch candidates (only 10 output channels fitted/routed). Add clearly marked test points, mounting holes, connector designators, power net names and zero-current control interfaces. 12 total pixel channels and ~6000 total pixels are DESIGN TARGETS, not proven firmware or electrical capabilities. This is a BENCH prototype schematic, not a certified road-lighting safety controller. Do not place schematic symbols or connect pads without verifying datasheet pinouts and dimensions. Do not make assumptions about the hiBCTR board's exact physical pin spacing or 5V power isolation.

## D. 12 pixel outputs
Place two Texas Instruments `SN74AHCT245` powered from logic 5V, with standard 0.1uF local decoupling. Configure DIR for A (ESP32 3.3V) to B (5V LED data); drive 12 A inputs from 12 unique native GPIOs. A1..A8 of U_BUF1 -> PIX_DATA_1..8; A1..A4 of U_BUF2 -> PIX_DATA_9..12. Tie unused A inputs to logic GND and leave unused B outputs open. Drive both active-low `/OE` pins with a normally-disabled transistor pull-down circuit: a pull-up to 5V at /OE; a 2N7002 with gate from GPIO44 through appropriate resistor and gate pulldown; MCU asserted high enables output by pulling `/OE` low, disabled on MCU reset. Verify behavior during split-power sequencing and boot. Buffer outputs -> appropriately reviewed small series damping resistors -> separate signal-only `PIX_DOUT_1..12` headers. DO NOT route LED +12V or LED return current through these signal headers. Put +12V/GND/DATA into final output harness/connectors using an adequately rated fused internal distribution bus, not the logic PCB. Add optional `BI_RESERVED_1..12` pads without connecting their electrical function until the exact WS2815 backup-data wiring is confirmed.
